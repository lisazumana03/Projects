About LG Games

At LG Games, we bring boys and girls of all age groups together to form one gaming community. We see gaming not only as entertainment, but as an adventure too.
The aim of this business is to break boundaries in gaming. During festive seasons or school holidays in general, we usually host FIFA tournaments so that every competitor can improve their gaming skill. We also ask permission from communities to use their halls to host a game rally so that we can try and reduce crime, dropping out of schools, substance abuse and poverty. We also offer learnerships to matrics who plan to take gap years or have a passion of helping children in terms of gaming. The rallies we host are opportunities for gamers to get to know each other which would mean we are enhacing better communication.
