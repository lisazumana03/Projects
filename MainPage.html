Welcome to my gaming website. This is where you can order your games online from sports, action and simulation (Game Genres) etc. You can find our shops in the mentioned locations of South Africa and in some countries of the Southern African Development Community (SADC) region, as our main headquarters are in Thembokwezi Square located in Mandalay, Cape Town. As a business we have a goal and that is to break boundaries in gaming, despite the background where kids come from or how old they are. In our shops, there are gaming rooms that are open to the youth, but they open 30 min after we set up and close 15 min before we end our day and this is where the youth can use it as a practice ground a few weeks before any gaming competition.
